# School System - Access Request Guide

## For Students, Teachers, and Staff

This guide explains how to request access to the school management system.

---

## Step-by-Step: Requesting Access

### Step 1: Navigate to Request Form

1. Visit the school's website/landing page
2. Look for the **"Request Access"** button in the navigation or hero section
3. Click the button to go to the request form

### Step 2: Fill Out Your Information

#### Personal Information Section
- **First Name**: Your first name (required)
- **Last Name**: Your last name (required)

#### Account Information Section
- **Email Address**: Your email (required)
  - This will be used to create your login username
  - Must be unique - no one else can use this email
- **Account Type**: Select your role (required)
  - Student
  - Teacher
  - Parent
  - Staff
  - Administrator

#### Contact Information Section
- **Phone Number**: Your phone number (optional)
  - Helps administrators contact you about your request

#### Request Details Section
- **Reason for Request**: Explain why you need access (required)
  - Example: "I am a new student enrolling in Grade 10"
  - Example: "I am a new teacher joining the Mathematics department"
  - Be as specific as possible to help admins approve your request

### Step 3: Review Your Information

Before submitting, double-check that:
- ✅ All required fields are filled
- ✅ Your email address is correct
- ✅ You selected the correct account type
- ✅ Your reason for access is clear

### Step 4: Submit Your Request

1. Click the **"Submit Request"** button (pink/neon button)
2. If there are any errors, you'll see them highlighted in red
3. Fix any errors and try again

### Step 5: Wait for Approval

1. You'll see a **success message** confirming your request was submitted
2. You'll be redirected to the login page
3. An administrator will review your request
4. **You will receive an email** when your request is approved or rejected

---

## What Happens After Submission?

### Timeline
- **Submitted**: Your request is in the system
- **1-2 Business Days**: Administrator reviews your request
- **Approved**: You receive login credentials via email
- **Login**: Use the credentials to access the system

### When Your Request is Approved

You will receive an **email with**:
- Username (usually based on your email)
- Temporary password
- Login instructions
- Link to the login page

### If Your Request is Rejected

You will receive an **email with**:
- Reason for rejection
- Instructions on next steps
- Contact information to reach the administrator

## Troubleshooting

### "An account with this email already exists"

**Problem**: The email you used is already registered in the system

**Solution**:
1. If this is your email, use the **"Sign In"** button instead
2. If you forgot your password, use **"Forgot Password"** on the login page
3. If this is not your email, contact the administrator

### "You have already submitted an access request"

**Problem**: You've already submitted a request with this email

**Solution**:
1. Your request is pending review
2. Please wait for administrator approval
3. You will receive an email once reviewed
4. Do not submit another request

### "Email field is required"

**Problem**: You left the email field empty

**Solution**:
1. Enter your email address
2. Make sure it's spelled correctly
3. Use an email you have access to

### Form won't submit

**Problem**: There are validation errors

**Solution**:
1. Check for red error messages below each field
2. Fill in all required fields (marked with *)
3. Make sure email address format is correct
4. Try again

## Important Information

### Privacy & Security
- Your information is kept confidential
- Only administrators can see access requests
- Your email will never be shared with third parties
- The password you receive should be changed on first login

### Email Address
- Must be a valid, working email
- Must be unique to the system
- Used as basis for your login username
- Keep it up to date so you receive notifications

### Account Type
- Select accurately - it determines what you can access
- Teachers have different permissions than students
- Your role can only be changed by administrators

### Request Reason
- Be clear and specific
- Helps administrators approve your request faster
- Use professional language

---

## Account Security After Approval

Once you receive your login credentials:

1. **Log in immediately**
2. **Change your password**:
   - Navigate to Profile → Change Password
   - Use a strong password (mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
3. **Update your profile**:
   - Add your phone number
   - Upload a profile picture
   - Update contact information
4. **Keep credentials secure**:
   - Don't share your username/password
   - Log out on shared computers
   - Never click links in suspicious emails

## FAQ

**Q: How long does approval take?**  
A: Typically 1-2 business days. Check your email for updates.

**Q: Can I request access for someone else?**  
A: No. Each person must submit their own request with their information.

**Q: What if I forgot my request was submitted?**  
A: Contact the administrator. They can check the status for you.

**Q: Can I change my request after submitting?**  
A: Contact the administrator with the changes needed.

**Q: What if I don't receive the approval email?**  
A: Check your spam/junk folder. Contact the administrator if still not found.

**Q: Can I request multiple account types?**  
A: Typically no. Request only the type you need. Contact admin if you need multiple.

**Q: What if my school email is different from my personal email?**  
A: Use the email that the administrator told you to use.
